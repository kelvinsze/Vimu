iOS & CarPlay Video In Car

Vimu

A lightweight local network video player and media receiver for personal video casting.

⚡ DLNA/UPnP Receiver

Standards-compliant SSDP device discovery and AVTransport renderer receiving streams directly from compatible third-party apps.

🚗 CarPlay Video

Native CarPlay integration allowing parked video playback on supported car multimedia units.

📺 Native AVPlayer Core

Hardware-accelerated playback for HLS (.m3u8), MP4, MOV with AirPlay support and responsive seeking.

Privacy & Compliance

Vimu is built for private and personal use. It requires no user account, contains no telemetry or third-party tracking SDKs, and accesses your local network purely for device discovery and direct media streaming.
